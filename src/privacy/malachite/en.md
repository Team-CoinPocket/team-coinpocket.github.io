# Privacy Policy — Malachite

Effective date: 2026-10-16

Malachite ("the Game") is made by CoinPocket ("we"). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect and why

We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and the Game has no account of its own.
The Game uses the following Google services, which collect information from your device on our behalf.

| Service | Information | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions | Showing ads, measuring ads, preventing fraud |
| Google Analytics for Firebase | App instance ID, gameplay events (for example: run started or ended, cards chosen, ads watched, purchases), device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address (country/region) | Understanding how the Game is played so we can improve balance and fix problems |
| Firebase Remote Config | Firebase installation ID, device and app information | Adjusting game settings without an app update, and comparing settings between groups of players (A/B tests) |
| Google Play Billing | Purchase records (product, time, status). We never receive your payment card details | Delivering in-app purchases |

Your game progress is stored on your device.

## Ads and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool.
You can change your choice later from the Game's settings.
Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is processed by Google as described above, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google may process this information on servers outside your country, including in the United States.

## Retention

Analytics data is kept according to the Google Analytics data retention setting for the Game (up to 14 months), and then deleted.
Information on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

## Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete information related to your device. Email us with the app instance ID shown in the Game's settings, and we will respond within 30 days.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
